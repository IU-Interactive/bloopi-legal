Privacy Policy — Bloopi

Last updated: 9 May 2026

Summary

Bloopi is designed for children ages 3 and up. We collect no personal data, no usage data, and no device identifiers. Bloopi works fully offline after installation. There is no Bloopi server.

What information we collect

None. Bloopi has no backend. The app stores some information locally on your child's device so the app can function, including:

  • Profile names, ages, and avatars you create for your child
  • Per-game progress and unlocked stickers
  • Daily playtime totals (used to enforce the playtime cap you set)
  • Bloopi+ entitlement status

This information stays on the device. It is never transmitted to us or to any third party.

What we do NOT collect

  • No name, email, password, or account information from parents
  • No location data
  • No device identifiers (advertising ID, device ID, IP address logs, etc.)
  • No camera or microphone access
  • No contacts, calendar, photos, or other personal files
  • No analytics, no telemetry, no crash reports sent to us

Third-party services

Bloopi uses Google Play's billing system for the optional Bloopi+ subscription. When a parent makes a purchase, Google handles that transaction directly. Google's privacy practices are described at policies.google.com/privacy.

We do not use any other third-party SDKs (no analytics, no ads, no social login, no crash reporting).

Children's privacy (COPPA / GDPR-K)

Bloopi is designed for use by children. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. Because we collect no information at all, there is nothing to delete on request. To remove the app's local data from your child's device, uninstall the app.

The Bloopi+ purchase flow lives behind a parent gate and requires parent involvement.

Subscription cancellation and refunds

Bloopi+ subscriptions are managed through your Google Play account. You can cancel at any time in Google Play → Subscriptions. Refund requests are handled by Google.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, the new version will appear at this URL with an updated date. We will not change the policy in a way that retroactively reduces your privacy.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact us at: support@bloopi.app
